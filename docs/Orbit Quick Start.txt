ORBIT - QUICK START
===================

Orbit is a SteamVR overlay: a watch on your wrist, your desktop monitors floating
in VR (with a laser mouse), a full VR keyboard and its own settings panel.


REQUIREMENTS
- Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, a DirectX 11 graphics card
- SteamVR. Overlays only show while the game runs through SteamVR.
  Virtual Desktop users: pick SteamVR as the runtime (not VDXR / OpenXR).


FIRST START
1. Start SteamVR. Orbit starts with it (tray icon), unless you switched that off
   in the installer.
2. The first time, Windows asks once for administrator rights. With them, the VR
   mouse and keyboard also work in admin windows (Task Manager, installers...).
   You can say No - everything else still works.
3. Your monitors appear in front of you as one curved screen.
   Turn your LEFT wrist towards your face to see the watch.


CONTROLS
- Point at a panel ................ laser; on a monitor it moves the real cursor
- Trigger ......................... click
- B / Y ........................... right click
- Stick up / down ................. scroll
- Grip on a panel ................. grab and move it
    while holding: stick up/down = distance, left/right = bend (monitors) or size
    other hand's grip on it too = resize with both hands
    hold B / Y when grabbing a monitor = take it out of its row
- Drop a monitor next to another .. it snaps into the row
- Both grips, twice quickly ....... hide / show monitors and keyboard
- Point at the watch with the other hand: keyboard, monitors on/off, recenter,
  layout profile (shows its name - press to switch), night mode (moon), settings
- Grab the watch with the other hand to move it on your wrist
  (Wrist > Lock position stops that, so it can't be nudged by accident)


SETTINGS
- In VR: the gear button on the watch opens the settings panel (grab it, close with X).
- On the desktop: click the tray icon (or start Orbit again from the Start menu).
- Tabs: Desktops (which monitors, size, curve, join/separate, smart frame rate),
  Windows (mirror single app windows), Layouts (Desk / Bed / Couch profiles),
  Wrist (watch face, 24-hour clock, frame-time graph), Keyboard (sounds, size,
  pop-up for text fields), Look (Modern or Windows 95 style, themes, any accent colour, panel colour, laser
  colour / thickness / always visible, night mode), General (laser smoothing,
  haptics, SteamVR start, admin, VRChat OSC, pause when the headset is off).


LAYOUT PROFILES (DESK / BED)
Each profile remembers where your monitors, windows and keyboard are, their size and
curve, and night mode. Switch with the layout button on the watch (it shows the name)
or under Layouts. The first time you switch to a profile, everything comes to where
you are looking - lying in bed, that is above you. Whatever you change afterwards is
kept in that profile when you switch away.


NIGHT MODE
The moon button on the watch (or Look > Night mode) dims every Orbit panel in VR;
Look > Night brightness sets how much.


SINGLE WINDOWS
Windows tab > Add a window > pick it from the list. The window floats in VR on its own,
follows it when it moves or resizes on the PC, and comes back by itself the next time
the program is open. Clicking it in VR brings it to the front on the PC first, so the
click lands in it (a minimised window is restored by the first click). Grip moves it,
the stick bends it, both hands resize it. Up to 6 windows.


WATCH FACES
Wrist > Watch face: Full (time, batteries, performance, music, buttons), Analog,
Minimal (big time + buttons) or Time (just the time). Performance: Numbers, or a Graph
of the last 5 seconds of frame times (yellow = reprojected, red = dropped).


SAVING POWER
- Smart frame rate (Desktops): monitors and windows you are not looking or pointing at
  update less often (15 fps at the edge of view, 4 fps behind you).
- Pause when the headset is off (General): taking the headset off stops all capturing
  until you put it back on.


KEYBOARD EXTRAS
- Pops up by itself when a click in VR lands in a text field (browser, Discord, search
  box...), and goes away when you click somewhere that is not text. Keyboard > Pop up
  for text fields switches this off.
- Top bar: Select all / Copy / Paste / Cut / Undo / Redo (Ctrl shortcuts).
- Globe key (next to space): switches the keyboard language. It cycles through the
  layouts installed in Windows, plus a built-in English (US) layout when English isn't
  installed (it types the characters directly, so it works with any Windows layout).
- "Chatbox" switches the keyboard to VRChat chatbox mode: type the line, Enter (or Send)
  posts it to the VRChat chatbox, Esc clears it. Needs OSC on in VRChat.


NOW PLAYING
The watch shows what is playing in Windows (Spotify, browser, ...) with previous /
play-pause / next buttons. Can be hidden under Wrist.


NOTIFICATIONS
Orbit shows notifications sent in XSOverlay's format (UDP port 42069 on this PC) as
cards in the lower part of your view - e.g. VRCX: Settings > Notifications > enable the
XSOverlay notifications. General > Notifications > Test shows a sample.
If XSOverlay itself is running it owns that port, then Orbit can't receive them.


VRCHAT AVATAR PARAMETERS (OSC)
Orbit sends the same avatar parameters as XSOverlay (isOverlayOpen, isKeyboardOpen,
isWristVisible, openOverlayCount, controller/tracker batteries) to VRChat on
127.0.0.1:9000, so avatar add-ons such as DXOverlay react to it.
Turn OSC on in VRChat's action menu. Can be switched off under General.


TROUBLESHOOTING
- Nothing in VR: is SteamVR running, and does the game use SteamVR?
- The cursor shakes: General > Smoothing > High.
- Clicks don't reach a window: turn on "Run as administrator" (General).
- Panels in a strange place: watch button "Recenter", or Desktops > Join into one
  curved screen.
- Lying down / reclined: look where you want the screens and press Recenter - when you
  look clearly up or down, everything is placed along your gaze (above you in bed).
- Settings and log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Orbit (config.json, orbit.log)


UPDATES
Settings > General > Check for updates. If a newer Orbit is out, the button turns into
"Update to ..." - Orbit downloads the new installer from GitHub, checks it, installs it over
itself (your settings are kept) and starts again.


UNINSTALL
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Orbit > Uninstall.
Your settings in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Orbit are kept unless you delete that folder.
