Free SteamVR overlay for Windows

Your desktop, a watch and a keyboard. Floating in VR.

OrbitOverlay puts a fully customizable watch on your wrist, your monitors and app windows around you as curved screens with a laser mouse, and a VR keyboard that shows up when you need it.

Features

Everything you reach for, without taking the headset off.

Turn your wrist to see the watch. Point at a screen to use your PC. Everything stays where you put it.

Wrist watch

A watch you design yourself

Build your face from widgets on a grid, give each one its own style and colour, and spread them over several pages. Tap any widget to open it big.

  • Clocks in many designs: flip, segment, words, Swiss station, squircle…
  • Batteries for headset, controllers and trackers
  • Now playing, volume, timer, stopwatch, calendar
  • Frame time, reprojection, GPU, CPU and memory
  • Microphone, notifications, button groups – and Minesweeper
Desktop in VR

Your monitors and windows, around you

Every monitor or single app window as a curved screen. The laser is your mouse: trigger to click, stick to scroll, grip to move. Smart frame rate keeps what you look at smooth.

Theater mode

One tap: your screen grows huge in front of you and the world dims behind it.

VR keyboard

Follows your Windows layout and pops up by itself when you click a text field.

VRChat

Made for VRChat nights

  • See who is in your instance and get a card when a friend arrives
  • Type straight into the chatbox, or show what you are listening to
  • No stray clicks: the game ignores your trigger while you point at a panel
  • XSOverlay-compatible notifications and avatar parameters

Layout profiles

Desk, Bed and Couch each remember where everything was. In bed, the screens come above you.

Stream dock

An Ajazz AKP03 run by Hexpad appears in VR: press keys and turn knobs with the laser.

Looks

Bento – or 1995

The modern Bento look with any accent colour, a font of your choice and night mode. Or switch everything – settings, watch, keyboard, notifications – to a pixel-perfect Windows 95 style.

Privacy

No account. No telemetry.

OrbitOverlay only talks to SteamVR and to apps on your own PC. The one internet request is the update check when you press it. Settings and logs stay on your computer.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Download

Get OrbitSetup.exe from the button above. It always points to the latest release.

Run the installer

It installs for your user only – no admin needed – and can start OrbitOverlay together with SteamVR.

Put on your headset

Turn your left wrist towards you to see the watch. Point at it with the other hand to use it.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info → Run anyway. The first start asks once for administrator rights so the VR mouse can click into admin windows – you can say no and everything else still works.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work with Quest, Pico or Virtual Desktop?

Yes, as long as the game runs through SteamVR. With Virtual Desktop, pick SteamVR as the runtime instead of VDXR / OpenXR, otherwise overlays can't show.

Why does it ask for administrator rights?

Only so the laser mouse can also click into windows that run as administrator. You can say no – everything else still works.

How do I update?

Settings › General › Check for updates. An installed copy updates itself from the GitHub releases, and a "What's new" window shows what changed.

Does it collect any data?

No. There is no account and no telemetry. The only internet request is the update check, and only when you press it.

Is it compatible with my XSOverlay add-ons?

Notifications use XSOverlay's format (UDP 42069), so apps like VRCX can send them, and the VRChat avatar parameters work with DXOverlay and similar add-ons.

How was it made?

OrbitOverlay is written largely with an AI assistant, from a human's ideas and testing in VR. It's open source under the MIT license, so you can read every line.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings › Apps › OrbitOverlay › Uninstall.
