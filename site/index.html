OrbitOverlay

A free SteamVR overlay for Windows: a watch on your wrist, your PC screens around you, and a keyboard when you need one.

Download OrbitOverlaySetup.exe latest release · Windows 10 / 11

Try it. This is the watch the way the app draws it. Swipe or tap the dots for the VRChat and Performance pages, tap a card to open it. The Minesweeper on page 2 is playable.

The clock is live. The batteries, frame times, the room and the song are the app's demo values.

What's in it

The watch

Look at your wrist. Fill a 6 × 4 grid with 32 widgets: clocks, batteries, music, frame times, buttons. Add more pages and swipe between them.

VRChat and Performance pages

Who is in the room, your microphone and notifications on one page, the last 30 seconds of frame times, CPU and VRAM on the next. Tap the room to see who is there.

A look for every widget

Tint, Solid, Two-tone, Header, Outline or Glass in 13 colours. Clocks as Flip, Stacked, Segment, Words or a station clock, and a font of your choice.

Desktop and keyboard

Monitors and windows as curved screens, the laser as your mouse, a keyboard that opens by itself. The bar under your middle monitor has Theater mode and the keyboard.

Minesweeper

A small tile that grows into the game: tap to open a square, hold to flag it. In the Windows 95 style it is the real 1995 Minesweeper.

Your look

Any accent and panel colour, night mode, or everything in Windows 95 style: the settings, the watch, the keyboard and Minesweeper.

Also: theater mode · Desk, Bed and Couch layouts · your song and time in the VRChat chatbox · XSOverlay-style notifications (VRCX) · the Hexpad stream dock on the watch · volume that works with Virtual Desktop · updates from inside the app · no telemetry

Install

  1. Download (49 MB) and run it. It installs for your Windows user, no admin rights needed.
  2. Put on your headset and look at your left wrist.

Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or 11, a DirectX 11 graphics card and SteamVR. With Virtual Desktop, pick SteamVR as the runtime. SmartScreen may warn, because the installer isn't signed yet: More info › Run anyway. Later updates come from Settings › General. Controls, questions and fixes are in the guide.
