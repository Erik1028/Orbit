OrbitOverlay

A free SteamVR overlay for Windows: a watch on your wrist, your PC screens around you, and a keyboard when you need one.

Download OrbitOverlaySetup.exe latest release · Windows 10 / 11

Try it. Tap a widget to open it, swipe for the second page.

Batteries, frame times and the song are demo values.

What's in it

The watch

Look at your wrist. Build the face from 24 widgets: clocks, batteries, music, frame times.

Desktop and keyboard

Monitors and windows as curved screens, the laser as your mouse, a keyboard that opens by itself.

Your look

Any accent colour, night mode, or everything in Windows 95 style.

Also: VRChat room and chatbox · XSOverlay notifications · Desk, Bed and Couch layouts · Hexpad stream dock · no telemetry

Install

  1. Download and run it. No admin rights needed.
  2. Put on your headset and look at your left wrist.

Windows 10 / 11 and SteamVR. SmartScreen may warn, because the installer isn't signed yet: More info › Run anyway. Controls, questions and fixes are in the guide.
