OrbitOverlay

A free SteamVR overlay for Windows. A watch on your wrist, your PC screens around you with the laser as a mouse, and a keyboard that shows up when you click a text field.

Download OrbitOverlaySetup.exe latest release · Windows 10 / 11

Try it. This is the custom watch face, rebuilt for the browser.

  • Tap a widget to open it big, like on your wrist
  • Swipe, or use the dots, for the second page
  • The timer, stopwatch and volume work
  • The moon button is night mode

Time and date are live. Batteries, frame times and the song are demo values; in VR they come from SteamVR and Windows.

The watch

Turn your wrist towards you and the watch appears. You build the face yourself from widgets on a 6 × 4 grid, give each one its own style and colour, and spread them over several pages. Tap a widget to open it big.

  • Clocks: analog, flip, segment, words, Swiss station
  • Battery for the headset, both controllers and trackers
  • Now playing, volume, timer, stopwatch, calendar
  • Frame time, reprojection, GPU, CPU and memory
  • Microphone, notifications, buttons, and Minesweeper

Your desktop in VR

Every monitor, or a single app window, becomes a curved screen you can place anywhere. Point at it and the laser moves your real mouse cursor. Smart frame rate keeps the screen you are looking at smooth.

Theater mode makes one screen huge and dims the world behind it.

Keyboard

It follows your Windows keyboard layout and opens by itself when you click into a text field. The shortcut bar has copy, paste and undo, and in chatbox mode it types straight into VRChat.

Settings and looks

Settings open on the desktop or as a panel in VR. Pick an accent colour, a panel colour, a theme and the laser colour. Night mode dims every panel and is one tap away on the watch.

If you prefer, the settings, watch, keyboard and notifications can all switch to a Windows 95 style, with bevelled buttons and a Date/Time window on your wrist.

Install

  1. Download OrbitOverlaySetup.exe. The link always points to the latest release.
  2. Run it. It installs for your Windows user only, without admin rights, and can start together with SteamVR.
  3. Put on your headset and look at your left wrist.

Windows SmartScreen may warn you, because the installer is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. On first start OrbitOverlay asks once for administrator rights, so the laser can also click into admin windows. You can say no and everything else still works.

Needs Windows 10 (2004 or later) or 11, a DirectX 11 GPU and SteamVR. With Virtual Desktop, choose SteamVR as the runtime. Something not working? See Troubleshooting.

Questions

Does it work with Quest, Pico or Virtual Desktop?
Yes, as long as the game runs through SteamVR. In Virtual Desktop, pick SteamVR instead of VDXR / OpenXR, otherwise overlays can't show.
How do I update?
Settings › General › Check for updates. The installed app updates itself from the GitHub releases and shows what changed.
Does it work with my XSOverlay add-ons?
Notifications use XSOverlay's format, and the VRChat avatar parameters work with DXOverlay and similar add-ons.
How was it made?
Largely written with an AI assistant, from my own ideas and a lot of testing in the headset. The whole source is on GitHub under the MIT license.
How do I uninstall it?
Windows Settings › Apps › OrbitOverlay › Uninstall. Your settings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Orbit.
Found a bug?
Open an issue on GitHub, and say which headset and version you use.
